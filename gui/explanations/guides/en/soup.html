Soup

Uncond-init ΔW soup — a quality-and-robustness recipe for a single-artist LoRA, not a new adapter family. Its output is an ordinary bakeable LoRA (output/ckpt/anima_soup_<target>.safetensors).

Three phases. (1) A short caption_dropout_rate 1.0 uncond inter-train on the artist shard that contains your target — a shared, deterministic checkpoint reused across every target in the same shard. (2) N seeded fine-tunes initialised from that uncond checkpoint. (3) An exact ΔW soup of those fine-tunes, SVD-truncated back to network_dim.

What it buys you. A quality win plus seed-lottery insurance: a soup of 3 fine-tunes lands at roughly the best single ingredient, so a bad seed draw can no longer sink the run. Rank-16 SVD truncation is near-free (~99.9% energy retained). It is not a memorization fix.

Requirements. Plain-LoRA checkpoints only (Hydra / Chimera are refused). The uncond pool must stay disjoint from your fine-tune images. Target is required — an artist directory under post_image_dataset/resized/. The pool, seeds and rank knobs have sensible defaults; leave them unless you know why. Runs as one daemon command job (the pipeline drives its own train.py phases). See docs/ and scripts/soup/.
